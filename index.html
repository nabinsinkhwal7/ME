About Me

Hello! I'm Nabin Sinkhwal, a passionate software developer who enjoys building digital solutions that simplify everyday life. From automating tasks like school billing to organizing information that is usually done manually, I love creating tools that make daily activities easier, more efficient, and more enjoyable.

My journey into technology started early—back in school, I was curious about how things worked and tried experimenting with small projects like cloning Facebook pages. Somehow, that curiosity eventually led me to receive a scholarship to study Computer Engineering. From there, my real journey in the tech world began. I'm still on that path, learning and growing every day, and excited for what's ahead.

When I'm not coding, I enjoy hiking, reading, and spending quality time with family and friends. I love learning from nature, exploring new places, and doing my part to respect and protect the environment. These moments keep me grounded and inspire my work as much as technology does.
